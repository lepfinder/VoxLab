# F4: TTS同级/更小替代选型

## Findings

### [1] Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512（2025/12）是目前 ≤0.5B 档中文竞争力最强的开源流式克隆 TTS：Apache-2.0、9 语种 + 18+ 中文方言、双流式首包约 150ms，CV3-Eval test-zh CER 1.21 / SS 78.0（RL 版 CER 0.81 / SS 77.4），优于同表 F5-TTS(0.3B)、Spark-TTS(0.5B)、CosyVoice2(0.5B)。
- quote: "Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512 | ✅ | 0.5B | 1.21 | 78.0 | ... Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512_RL | ✅ | 0.5B | 0.81 | 77.4"
- url: https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-12
- confidence: high

### [2] CosyVoice 系已具备完整本地生产栈：CosyVoice2/3 支持 vLLM 0.9/0.11+、Triton+TensorRT-LLM（约 4× 加速）、gRPC/FastAPI Docker runtime；CosyVoice2 官方表 Seed-TTS test-zh CER 1.45 / SS 75.7，test-hard CER 6.83 / SS 72.4。
- quote: "Using TensorRT-LLM to accelerate cosyvoice2 llm could give 4x acceleration comparing with huggingface transformers implementation."
- url: https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-12
- confidence: high

### [3] F5-TTS Base（约 0.3B，DiT+CFM）代码 MIT、权重因 Emilia 为 CC-BY-NC；有官方 MLX 移植（f5-tts-mlx）与 ONNX Runtime 版；L20+TRT-LLM 并发 2 时 RTF 0.0394、平均延迟 253ms（16 NFE）。Seed-TTS test-zh CER 1.52 / SS 74.1（CosyVoice 表）。
- quote: "Our code is released under MIT License. The pre-trained models are licensed under the CC-BY-NC license due to the training data Emilia"
- url: https://github.com/SWivid/F5-TTS
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-03-12
- confidence: high

### [4] f5-tts-mlx 在 Apple Silicon 上可直接 pip 安装并支持 4/8-bit 量化，M3 Max 上样例约 4 秒生成；ONNX 版见 DakeQQ/F5-TTS-ONNX——F5-TTS 是本批模型中 Mac/MLX+ONNX 生态最完整的 ≤500M 选项。
- quote: "You can listen to a sample here that was generated in ~4 seconds on an M3 Max MacBook Pro. ... 4-bit and 8-bit variants are supported."
- url: https://github.com/lucasnewman/f5-tts-mlx
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [5] Spark-TTS-0.5B 基于 Qwen2.5、无 flow-matching、Apache-2.0，支持中英零样本克隆与性别/音高/语速可控生成；Triton+TRT-LLM 并发 2 时 RTF 0.0737。但 CosyVoice/VoxCPM 官方表均显示其说话人相似度偏弱（Seed-TTS test-zh SS 仅 66.0，test-en SS 57.3）。
- quote: "Built entirely on Qwen2.5, Spark-TTS eliminates the need for additional generation models like flow matching. ... Spark TTS | ✅ | 0.5B | 1.2 | 66.0 | 1.98 | 57.3"
- url: https://github.com/SparkAudio/Spark-TTS
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-03-12
- confidence: high

### [6] IndexTTS-2.5（B 站，2026/08/10）为 0.8B、支持中/英/日/西/阿 + 拼音/CMU/假名发音控制 + 语速 duration_factor + 情感向量/参考音频/文本情感，官方 vLLM recipe；RTX 4090 上 kv_cache 下整体 RTF 约 0.21（bf16）。CV3-Eval test-zh WER 4.36 / SS 77.10（RL 版 3.93 / 77.92）。许可证为 bilibili Model Use License，商用需联系 indexspeech@bilibili.com。
- quote: "IndexTTS-2.5 ... supports Chinese, English, Japanese, Spanish and Arabic, with fine-grained emotion control, speaking speed control, pronunciation control (Pinyin / CMU phonemes / Japanese Kana), and faster inference than IndexTTS-2."
- url: https://github.com/index-tts/index-tts
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-08-10
- confidence: high

### [7] IndexTTS-2（1.5B，2025/09）在 Seed-TTS 上中文很强：test-zh CER 1.03 / SS 76.5、test-hard CER 7.12 / SS 75.5，优于 CosyVoice2、F5-TTS、Spark-TTS；若接受 1.5B 档它是中文克隆质量优先的候选，但非 ≤500M。
- quote: "Index-TTS2 | ✅ | 1.5B | 1.03 | 76.5 | 2.23 | 70.6 | 7.12 | 75.5"
- url: https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-08
- confidence: high

### [8] Fish Audio S2 Pro（2026，Dual-AR：Slow-AR 4B + Fast-AR 400M）在 Seed-TTS Eval 取得开源/闭源全场最低 WER（zh 0.54% / en 0.99%），SGLang 下 H200 RTF 0.195、TTFA ~100ms、80+ 语种、10-30s 克隆；但主干 4B 超出 ≤2B 预算，且权重为 FISH AUDIO RESEARCH LICENSE（非标准开源）。
- quote: "S2-Pro | 4B parameters ... Seed-TTS Eval — WER (Chinese) 0.54% (best overall) ... Real-Time Factor (RTF): 0.195 ... Time-to-First-Audio (TTFA): ~100 ms"
- url: https://github.com/fishaudio/fish-speech
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026
- confidence: high

### [9] GPT-SoVITS（MIT，61.7k stars）仍是 few-shot 微调克隆生态最成熟的选择：1 分钟数据微调、5s 零样本、中/英/日/韩/粤；v2Pro/v4 已发布，官方 RTF：4060Ti 0.028、4090 0.014、M4 CPU 0.526。参数量远小于 500M，适合高保真定制音色而非通用零样本。
- quote: "RTF(inference speed) of GPT-SoVITS v2 ProPlus: 0.028 tested in 4060Ti, 0.014 tested in 4090 ... 0.526 in M4 CPU."
- url: https://github.com/RVC-Boss/GPT-SoVITS
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [10] VoxCPM2（OpenBMB，2026/04，2B，Apache-2.0 可商用）为 tokenizer-free 扩散自回归，30 语种 + 9 中文方言、Voice Design、可控克隆、原生 48kHz；Seed-TTS test-zh CER 0.97 / SS 79.5、test-EN WER 1.84 / SIM 75.3；RTX 4090 RTF ~0.30，Nano-vLLM/vLLM-Omni 加速至 ~0.13，另有 llama.cpp-omni GGUF（M4 Pro Metal RTF ~1.76）。旧版 VoxCPM-0.5B/1.5B（0.5–0.8B）zh CER 0.93–1.18 / SS 77.0–77.2，VRAM 5–6GB。
- quote: "VoxCPM2 | 2B | ✅ | 1.84 | 75.3 | 0.97 | 79.5 | 8.13 | 75.3 ... RTF as low as ~0.13 on NVIDIA RTX 4090"
- url: https://github.com/OpenBMB/VoxCPM
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04
- confidence: high

### [11] ChatTTS 仍是对话韵律向的轻量选项（约 4GB VRAM、4090 上 RTF ~0.3），中英、细粒度 laugh/uv_break 控制；但开源 40k 小时底模被人为加噪+MP3 压缩，代码 AGPL-3.0、模型 CC BY-NC 4.0，且 roadmap 中的流式与零样本推理未在主 README 宣告完成，不适合商用克隆主路径。
- quote: "For a 30-second audio clip, at least 4GB of GPU memory is required. For the 4090 GPU ... The Real-Time Factor (RTF) is around 0.3. ... The model is published under CC BY-NC 4.0 license."
- url: https://github.com/2noise/ChatTTS
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [12] MaskGCT（~1B，Amphion/ICLR 2025）为全非自回归 mask-and-predict，Seed-TTS test-zh SIM-O 0.774 / WER 2.273（gt-length 2.183），开源可本地跑但无流式、无 MLX/ONNX 官方支持；2025/02 后续为 Metis（多任务统一框架）。在 VoxCPM 官方对照中 SIM 与 CER 均弱于同代 0.5B 级 CosyVoice2/VoxCPM-0.5B。
- quote: "MaskGCT is a fully non-autoregressive TTS model that eliminates the need for explicit alignment information ... MaskGCT | 0.774(0.805) | 2.273(0.843)"
- url: https://github.com/open-mmlab/Amphion/tree/main/models/tts/maskgct
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-10-19
- confidence: high

### [13] Orpheus-TTS（Llama-3B，Apache-2.0）强项是英语情感/流式（~200ms，可降至 ~100ms），2025/04 才放出多语种 research preview；主模型为英文，无中文官方 MOS/SS 数字，3B 超出 ≤500M 档，中文场景不作为优先替代。
- quote: "Orpheus TTS is a SOTA open-source text-to-speech system built on the Llama-3b backbone. ... Low Latency: ~200ms streaming latency"
- url: https://github.com/canopyai/Orpheus-TTS
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-04
- confidence: high

### [14] 第三方官方对照表（VoxCPM2 / CosyVoice3 / IndexTTS2.5 README）交叉一致显示：≤2B 开源中文第一梯队为 CosyVoice3-0.5B(0.5B)、VoxCPM2(2B)、IndexTTS2.5(0.8B)、Qwen3-TTS(1.7B，已在役)、FireRedTTS-2(1.5B)、GLM-TTS(1.5B)、LongCat-Audio-DiT(3.5B，超预算但 zh SS 79.7 最高)；MegaTTS3(0.5B) 数字好但未开源权重。
- quote: "MegaTTS3 | 0.5B | ❌ | 2.79 | 77.1 | 1.52 | 79.0 ... LongCat-Audio-DiT | 3.5B | ✅ | 1.50 | 78.6 | 1.09 | 81.8"
- url: https://github.com/OpenBMB/VoxCPM
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-04
- confidence: medium
